Block 2 · The idea explained

What it is

What is a coverage model?

It is how a company reaches its customers in a market: who sells, to whom, through which channel, and with what support. There are three basic answers.

Direct

Your own team sells to the customer and serves them.

Fits concentrated demand, complex products and markets large enough to carry a local team.

Indirect

Partners sell and serve on your behalf.

Fits dispersed demand, and markets where local relationships and tenders decide the result.

Hybrid

You keep key accounts or key functions. Partners cover the rest.

Fits markets with a few large buyers and a long tail of smaller ones.
