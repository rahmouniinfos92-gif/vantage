What we do

Connecting global business ambitions with local market realities.

Vantage Advisory works with MedTech companies, investors and healthcare organizations to address commercial challenges, strengthen market presence and support sustainable business growth.

We combine international business experience with an understanding of local market dynamics, bringing senior-level insight and practical support to every engagement.

We bring structure where markets are complex: a clear picture of where you stand, a vision of where to go, and a roadmap to get there.

Commercial strategy & coverage expansion

Our specialty: growth in decentralized, fragmented and complex markets. We turn the market into a clear plan: where you stand, which territories to prioritize, and the roadmap to grow coverage and revenue. Direct, indirect or hybrid, in mature and growing markets alike.

Go-to-market strategy

Entry and launch plans grounded in how each market really buys: regulation, reimbursement, tenders, and the clinicians who shape adoption. Each plan starts with a clear valuation of the market opportunity.

Commercial operations & partner frameworks

We restructure commercial operations, policies and processes. This includes an indirect business management framework and a partner management framework, both strategic and operational, built for your organization.

Organization & operating model

Clear roles between headquarters, regions and local teams, so decisions are made faster and closer to the customer.

M&A & commercial due diligence

Commercial due diligence before a deal and a focused growth plan after it, for private equity and strategic investors.

Health systems & medical technology advisory

Independent advice for hospital groups, health authorities and NGOs on equipment strategy, procurement and lifetime cost.

FAQ

Questions we often hear

How is Vantage different from a large consulting firm?

We’re a small, senior-led firm. The people you meet do the work, and all of us have run MedTech businesses ourselves. We take on a limited number of engagements so each one gets our full attention.

Where do you work?

We’re an international firm. Africa and the Middle East, including the GCC, are our core regions. We also work across Central & Eastern Europe, Latin America and Asia Pacific, in emerging and mature markets alike.

Which sectors do you cover?

MedTech and life sciences: in vitro diagnostics, imaging, medical equipment, consumables and healthcare infrastructure. On request, we also support companies in other industries with commercial operations restructuring and international expansion.

How does an engagement start?

With a conversation. If there’s a fit, we propose a short first phase with a defined scope, so you can judge our work before committing to more.

Is our conversation confidential?

Always. We’re glad to sign an NDA before you share anything sensitive.

Will changing our partner model damage our distributor relationships?

The aim is the opposite. We build plans that work for both sides: clearer commitments for you, and a stronger business for your partner.
